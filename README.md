# Bank Account Analyzer

A simple, offline, privacy-first tool for analyzing bank statements and identifying recurring costs.

## Features

- 📊 **Recurring Cost Detection**: Identifies regular expenses based on a customizable threshold
- 🏧 **ATM Withdrawal Tracking**: Automatically detects and separates ATM withdrawals
- 🔒 **100% Private**: All processing happens locally in your browser - no data is ever sent anywhere
- 📋 **Copy-Paste Ready**: Works with standard bank statement formats
- 🎯 **Smart Parsing**: Handles Italian number formatting (e.g., -1.111,11)

## How It Works

1. Copy your bank statement rows
2. Set your cost threshold
3. Click "Analyze & Calculate"
4. View your recurring costs, ATM withdrawals, and net recurring expenses

## Why This Tool?

Many people need to understand their recurring expenses to better manage their finances. This tool helps you:
- Identify fixed monthly costs
- Separate ATM withdrawals from other expenses
- Get a clear picture of your regular financial commitments

## Privacy First

- ❌ No data collection
- ❌ No external API calls
- ❌ No tracking
- ✅ All processing is local

## Usage

1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Copy your bank statement data
3. Paste it into the text area
4. Click "Analyze & Calculate"
